# Recording alignment revision

The 80-slide lecture retains 40 distinct embedded recordings: **26 newly sourced recordings and 14 retained recordings**, assigned to their directly matching material. Twenty are classified as academic and twenty as observational in the internal manifest. Only one USC vocal-tract MRI remains, on articulatory movement (slide 10).

Slide 46 now uses a real patient reading examination during occipitotemporal electrical stimulation. Other additions include nonfluent and fluent aphasia examinations, poststroke language testing, signed-language stimuli, word-selective cortical localization, diffusion tractography, and clinical speech-decoding demonstrations. Each caption names what is shown; notes explain what students should observe.

Recordings follow concepts rather than a uniform interval: quarters contain 12/7/7/14 recordings; the single four-slide video-free interval covers slides 42–45. All original teaching paragraphs, titles, figures, and the 80-slide sequence are preserved.

Every source was checked against its original preparation. Some recordings illustrate a mechanism rather than reproduce the adjacent study; the source reference makes that distinction explicit. Original scientific images and recordings were not generated. Three unusually large clinical recordings were compressed at native resolution and timing; whole-movie SSIM exceeds 0.994 and the original audio packets are identical. The remaining 37 movies retain original published bytes. Full-source books and articles remain private.
