## Slide 4: Calculated stress after mouse-brain impact. Rene Solano Fonseca (2021).

Strain describes a change in a material's shape or length relative to its starting state. Stress describes the internal force acting through an area of material. A short, rapidly applied deformation can disturb axons, membranes, and vessels even without a large visible wound or a skull fracture.

## Slide 6: Cultured axon during controlled stretch. Sushil Dubey (2020).
