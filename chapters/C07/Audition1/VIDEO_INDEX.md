Thirty-seven MP4 recordings retain the original publisher bytes. Two older MOV files required H.264 compatibility conversion without changing resolution, frame rate, or audio; one M4V was losslessly remuxed to MP4. Source and embedded hashes, dimensions, timing, codecs, and audio are in media_sources.json. The lossless remux preserves all 1,219 video packets and their presentation timestamps; its container-reported duration differs by less than one nominal frame. Every complete embedded recording passed FFmpeg decoding.
