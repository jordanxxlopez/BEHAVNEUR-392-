# From Reproduction to Care: Hormones, Social Circuits, and Parenting

Chapter 10 contains 80 slides: title, 78 teaching slides, and six exam-level takeaways on the last slide. Coverage includes reproductive differentiation, endocrine feedback and cycles, mating circuits, human sex traits and orientation, pair bonding, parental motivation, infant cues, oxytocin-dependent learning, paternal and alloparental care.

There are 31 foundation slides and 47 research slides, with original source imagery on every teaching slide. Forty distinct original recordings are embedded, internally 20 academic and 20 aesthetic, ten per teaching quarter and six in the final ten teaching slides. All 234 substantive paragraphs have matching teaching transcripts in notes. Notes also contain full references, original figure legends, recording identities, source credits, licenses, and preparation distinctions.

The editable PowerPoint is `From_Reproduction_to_Care_Chapter_10.pptx`. The `_Static.pdf` companion retains original recording posters. Arial is used throughout. The GitHub media download serves the actual PowerPoint rather than a Git LFS pointer.

Validation records: VALIDATION.json, paragraph_line_check.json, media_validation.json. Every slide and source panel was visually reviewed. All original recordings were fully decoded with FFmpeg. Native PowerPoint movie relationships, timing, and embedded hashes were checked. Desktop PowerPoint playback has not been tested.

Rebuild with write_content.py, assemble_spec.py, repository tools/build_lecture.py, and embed_media.py; render with make_static_pdf.py. Run the actual PDF paragraph-line audit and both repository validators after any changes. Presenton supplies a static PDF companion and preview; these API links expire after 24 hours. Full source books and articles remain private.
