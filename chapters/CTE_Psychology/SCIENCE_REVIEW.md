# Standalone lecture: cognitive and psychological interpretation

This new 80-slide lecture was requested separately from the previously delivered neurobiology lecture. The first lecture is preserved. Foundations concern cognition, psychological symptoms, clinical history, and imaging interpretation; primary research includes football cohorts and directly taught human cognitive-neuroscience experiments.

Public player records are dated and distinguished from confirmed pathology. Webster, Seau, Stabler, and Gifford have publicly reported postmortem findings. Buoniconti's 2017 report describes symptoms and a donation pledge rather than declaring a diagnosis in that report. Favre's disclosed Parkinson disease diagnosis is not relabeled CTE. McMahon's reported memory experiences are not a tissue diagnosis. Namath's photograph illustrates football expertise and does not imply a clinical condition. No private medical records or inferred individual diagnoses are included.

Clinical teaching recordings are explicitly separate from football participants. A clinical error is not labeled as evidence of CTE in the person filmed. Original recordings show the actual cognitive operation described: memory questions, digit span, word generation, reasoning, spatial drawing, recognition, praxis, or a specified human laboratory task. Original imaging visualizations retain the authors' displays and are not generated here.

Structural MRI, diffusion MRI, fMRI, MEG, EEG, TMS-EEG, and PET measure different signals. Group differences, associations with exposure, prediction of a clinical syndrome, and validation against tissue pathology are separate claims. Tractography does not photograph individual axons; electrical-phase and fMRI colors are not lesion maps. Source illustrations from unrelated clinical populations are named as such.

The 2021 TES criteria are a research framework and do not establish CTE during life. The 2014 criteria validation is described as an analysis of those older criteria, not the revised criteria. MRI and flortaucipir findings are not advertised as validated individual CTE tests. The 2026 pTau217 study primarily tracked Alzheimer-related biology. Alzheimer disease and other contributors may coexist with a football exposure history.

The lecture includes findings without increased measured risk as well as findings of worse outcomes. National adolescent follow-up, youth exposure, former professionals, selected symptomatic volunteers, and brain donors are not interchangeable populations. Quantities and statistical bookkeeping are omitted from teaching prose where they do not explain a concept.

Experimental stimulation in healthy volunteers is not presented as a CTE treatment. Correlations involving CSF cytokines or neurotransmitter metabolites are not described as demonstrated treatment targets. Clinical care can address symptoms, function, pain, sleep, and other identified conditions without waiting for tissue confirmation.

Full source legends, study citations, public player reports, image credits, licenses, and movie provenance are retained in the manifests and notes. No full textbook PDF is published.
