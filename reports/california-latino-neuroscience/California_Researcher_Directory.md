# California neuroscience researcher directory

Checked 13 October 2026. The core list has 19 researchers: 17 publicly recognized in the Hispanic/Latinx Atlas and two included using explicitly documented Latin American national-origin biographies. The latter are labeled and are not claims of personal self-identification. Two related-field lab leaders and one Spain-origin PI appear separately. Nineteen unresolved leads are not classified as confirmed Latino PIs. This is a source-backed, broad search result, not a complete statewide census.

Activity evidence A = an accessible lab, team or institutional page explicitly describes the independent group; B = converging current faculty/group and research evidence, with lab page blocked. These grades describe evidence quality, not scientific merit. No hiring availability is inferred. Less direct activity evidence is retained in the follow-up section outside the active count.

Background evidence is independent of activity evidence. No surnames, photos, language ability or participant populations were used to assign identity.

## Coverage and remaining gaps

The search examined California candidates across the Bay Area, Davis/Sacramento, the Central Valley, greater Los Angeles, the Inland Empire and San Diego. It used the statewide subset of the Atlas, institutional pages, direct web searches and primary-paper records. Stanford, Caltech, UC Santa Barbara, UC Irvine, UC Santa Cruz, Salk and Scripps remain areas for further verification; a missing confirmed entry is not proof that the institution has no Latino neuroscience PIs. Search-engine rate limits and blocked profile pages limited some follow-up searches.
